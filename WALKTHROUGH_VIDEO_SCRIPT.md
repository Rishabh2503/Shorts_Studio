# Shorts Studio Walkthrough Video Script (2:30)

Use this script to record a concise onboarding video and save it as `public/walkthrough.mp4`.

## 0:00 - 0:15 Intro
- Show homepage and top controls.
- Voice: "Welcome to Shorts Studio. In this quick walkthrough, you will create a full short video from prompt to export in under three minutes."

## 0:15 - 0:40 Generate visuals
- Open Media & AI Generate panel.
- Enter prompt and click Generate.
- Voice: "Start with AI image generation. Describe your scene, choose a style, and generate. Each image is added as a timeline clip."

## 0:40 - 1:00 Upload media
- Use Upload image/video.
- Voice: "You can also drag and drop your own images or videos. Videos are sampled into editable timeline clips."

## 1:00 - 1:25 Add audio and sync
- Add main audio and optional background music.
- Voice: "Add voiceover in the main audio track and optional background music in the second track. Then choose sync mode based on your editing style."

## 1:25 - 1:50 Captions
- Open Script panel, run transcription, show caption style.
- Voice: "Generate AI captions on-device using Whisper, edit words if needed, and customize style, color, and position."

## 1:50 - 2:10 Timeline polish
- Reorder clips, apply split-screen or effects.
- Voice: "Polish pacing by dragging clips to reorder, adjusting durations, and using split-screen layouts for comparisons."

## 2:10 - 2:25 Export
- Click Render & download.
- Voice: "Preview, then click Render and Download. The app records in real-time for reliable audio sync and saves a WebM ready for upload."

## 2:25 - 2:30 Outro
- Voice: "That is it. You are ready to publish high-retention Shorts with Shorts Studio."

## Recording settings
- Resolution: 1920x1080
- FPS: 30
- Cursor highlight: ON
- Mic noise suppression: ON
- Keep recording under 3 minutes
